04The verdict

Nobody else occupies this square.

The hosted five give up openness and self-containment; the open-source three give up framework freedom, completeness, or upkeep. MailCraft alone holds all four — and when you want more than email, the platform it comes from is one free sign-up away.

MIT — actually openThe entire editor is source you can read, fork and ship — not an open wrapper around a closed iframe.
Framework-agnosticOne Web Component: the same tag in React, Angular, Vue, Svelte, or a plain <script>.
Self-containedZero dependencies, no vendor cloud, no metering. Runs from a file:// page.
